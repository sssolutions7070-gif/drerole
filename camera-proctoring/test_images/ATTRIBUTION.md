# Test image attribution

These images are used only to verify the face/phone detectors in `verify_models.py`; they are
not shipped as part of the running application.

## face_sample.jpg

The "Lena" image, a standard computer-vision test image used across the field for decades and
redistributed by OpenCV itself in its own samples
(`opencv/opencv/samples/data/lena.jpg`, https://github.com/opencv/opencv). Included here under
the same terms OpenCV distributes it.

## phone_sample.jpg

Resized (longest side 1200px, JPEG quality 90) from "Smartphone with case cover on table.jpg" by

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Smartphone_with_case_cover_on_table.jpg, licensed under
